# Security policy

Verilay is a small, independently built project. If you think you have found a security problem in **verilay.dev** or in this repository, I would like to hear about it.

## How to report
- **Email:** moses@verilay.dev, with the subject "Security report".
- Or use GitHub's private route: **Security, then Report a vulnerability** on this repository (if you see that button).
- Please include what you found, where, and the steps to see it for yourself. A screenshot helps.
- **Please do not post it publicly first.** Give me a reasonable chance to fix it.

## What to expect
I read every report. I will reply as soon as I can, usually within a few days, tell you whether I can reproduce it, and let you know when it is fixed. If you want to be credited, say so.

## Please
- Test only with your own accounts and your own data.
- Do not access, change or delete anyone else's data.
- Do not run denial-of-service or heavy automated attacks, and do not use social engineering.
- Stop and tell me if you reach data that is not yours.

I will not take action against anyone who follows these rules and reports in good faith.

## In scope
- The live site at https://verilay.dev and its pages
- The code in this repository

## Out of scope
- Problems in other people's apps that Verilay has scanned (report those to their owners)
- Findings that need a stolen device, a hacked inbox, or an outdated browser
- Missing "best practice" headers with no real impact

There is no paid bounty programme. Thank you for helping keep Verilay and its users safe.
